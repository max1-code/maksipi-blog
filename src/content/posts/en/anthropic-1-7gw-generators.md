Anthropic has closed a roughly $3.4 billion deal for 1.7 gigawatts of Caterpillar gas generators through OnePWR. This is not a model announcement. It is a physical infrastructure announcement.

### What they are actually buying

Not just generation capacity. They are buying deployment speed and reduced regulatory risk. AI data centers are no longer waiting for the traditional grid to catch up. They prefer to bring the power plant with them.

1.7 GW is not a small number. It is on the order of what a mid-sized city consumes. And they are solving it with gas, not with five-year renewable promises.

### The repeating pattern

OpenAI, Microsoft, Google and now Anthropic are converging on the same conclusion: the bottleneck has shifted from chips to power and permits. Anyone who does not solve electricity supply in 2026-2028 stays out of the next training wave.

GPU prices remain high, but predictable. The price of not having power on time is existential.

### What it means for everyone else

If you are a mid-sized lab or a company training your own models, this move changes the math. Reserving H100s or B200s is no longer enough. You need a credible megawatt plan. And that plan, today, often runs through self-generation or long-term contracts that few can sign.
