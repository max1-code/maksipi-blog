Two moves from the same weekend confirm the same diagnosis: the limit is no longer model architecture. It is electrons and silicon.

### Anthropic is not waiting for the grid

The lab closed a $3.4 billion deal for 1.7 gigawatts of Caterpillar gas generators. It did not buy an energy company. It bought equipment orders and installation capacity. It is the most direct way to skip years of grid interconnection queues.

1.7 GW is not residual capacity. It is the scale of a mid-sized city. And they solved it with gas, not with five-year renewable promises.

### Google answers with custom silicon

In the same cycle Google introduced two new eighth-generation TPU variants: 8tx (confidential compute) and 8i Max (double compute and memory density for long contexts). Four distinct systems in the same generation in a single year. The message is clear: a generic accelerator is no longer enough. You have to build the chip for each stage of the agentic lifecycle.

### The consolidating pattern

OpenAI, Microsoft, Google and Anthropic are converging. Reserving H100s or B200s is no longer enough. You need a credible megawatt plan and, increasingly, custom silicon or priority access to it. Anyone who does not solve both in 2026-2028 stays out of the next wave of large-scale training.
